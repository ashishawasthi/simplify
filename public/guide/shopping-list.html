📝 Make a shopping list

Plan what to buy, and check it fits the money you have — your budget.

  1. Under My money, type how much you can spend.
  2. Under I need to buy, type the name of a thing and its price. You can leave the name empty. Tap ＋ Add item for the next one.
  3. Read the answer at the bottom.
✅ Yes! Within your budget — it also shows the money left.
✋ Over your budget — it shows how much too much. Take something off the list.
Example: My money $5.00. Flour $1.20, Chocolate chips $0.80, Milk $1.50 → Yes! Within your budget. Money left: $1.50

The name boxes use your normal keyboard, so you can also say a name with the keyboard's microphone.
