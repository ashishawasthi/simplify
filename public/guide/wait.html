Wait

Shows how long a wait is. The blue part gets smaller as the time goes.

  1. Tap 1, 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  2. Watch the blue disc. The time left is under it in words.
  3. At the end, the disc turns green: ✔ Done.
  • Tap the disc to pause it. Tap it again to go on.
  • The round ✕ at the top right stops the wait. Put it back brings back the time that was left.
  • While I wait: tap ＋ Add to show up to 3 pictures of things to do while waiting.
  • Sound is off at first. Turn it on for one soft sound at the end. A phone that can buzz also buzzes.
Keep the app on the screen while you wait. A web app cannot sound an alarm or show a message when it is closed, in the background, or when the screen is locked. The screen stays on by itself while a wait runs. If you leave the app, come back to see the time left — it is still right.

In I need, the Break card opens a 2 or 5 minute wait.
