💲 Next dollar

Not sure how to pay the exact amount? Pay the next whole dollar instead.

  1. Under Things I buy, type the price of each thing. Tap ＋ Add item for the next one.
  2. The app adds them up for you: Things cost.
  3. The answer at the bottom shows the next dollar to pay, and the change you get back.
Example: $1.20 + $0.80 + $1.50 = $3.50 → Next dollar: $4. You get back 50¢.

If the things cost a whole number of dollars, like $4.00, the answer is that same amount: it is exact, so there is no change.
