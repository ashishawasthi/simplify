🔒 Privacy: what stays on the device

Everything stays on your phone or iPad: your money and prices, lists, cards, the pictures you picked, and the set-up. There is no account and no sign-in, and nothing tracks what you do.

Nothing a student taps is recorded — not which card, not when, not where it hurts.

My class 🔗

Only on a device that follows a class. The app downloads the class's page, with its pictures and videos. To do that it sends only the class code — and, like any website, the device's internet address. Nothing the student types or taps is sent.

A YouTube video loads nothing until someone taps it. After the tap, YouTube (Google) gets the device's internet address and which video is watched.

Shared devices 🔗

Sharing an iPad or phone with other people? Tap the round ✕ at the top right of each tool you used when you finish. Then the next person starts with empty boxes. Keep personal details, like a stop near home, off a shared iPad.

The pictures 🔗

Many pictures come from Noto Emoji by Google, used under the Apache License 2.0 (licence). The Singapore ones — the HDB block, the bus, the MRT train, the tray return and more — are drawn for Simplify.
