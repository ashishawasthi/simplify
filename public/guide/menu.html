🏠 The menu

The app opens on its menu: six big buttons, one for each money tool.

  1. Tap the tool you want.
  2. To go back, tap 🏠 Menu at the top left. On Android, the back button works too.
💡 Each tool remembers its own numbers. Try another tool, come back, and your numbers are still there.

Start over, at the top right, empties only the tool you are in. A Put it back button shows for a few seconds, in case you did not mean to.

For teachers: every tool has its own link, like simplify.whiz.coach/#next-note. Share it to open that tool straight away.
