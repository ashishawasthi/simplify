🛒 Can I buy?

Check if you have enough money for the things you want. Tap Can I buy? on the app's menu to start.

💰 1. Put in your money 🔗

Tap the My money box at the top and type how much money you have. That is all you need to do — the answer works itself out at the bottom of the screen.

Not sure how much you have? Count your notes and coins with the 💵 button. Rather talk than type? Say the amount with the 🎤.

🛒 2. Add the things you want to buy 🔗

Under Things I want to buy, type the price of the thing you want. The price is on the tag or the shelf.

Want more than one thing? Tap ＋ Add item and type the next price. That button only appears once the last price box has something in it, so you never end up with empty boxes.

✅ 3. Read the answer 🔗

As soon as you type a price, the answer shows at the bottom of the screen. It updates by itself — you do not need to press anything.

✅ Green means CAN BUY — you have enough money. It also shows how much money you will have left.
✋ Red means CANNOT BUY — it shows how much more money you need.

The keyboard can cover the bottom of the screen while you type. The answer shows at the top too, between 🏠 and ✕: like ✋ $7.40 more or ✅ $32.60 left.

If the My money box is empty, the app counts it as $0. So the answer says Cannot buy until you put your money in.

💵 4. Not enough? See the money you need 🔗

When the answer says Cannot buy, tap 💵 Show me. The app shows the notes and coins you still need, with pictures. Tap ✔ OK to go back.

Example: you have $5 and the thing costs $6.30. You need $1.30 more: $1 + 20¢ + 10¢.

Made a mistake in one box? Tap the ✕ inside that box to empty it. To clear everything at once, tap the round ✕ at the top right. Either way a Put it back button appears for a few seconds, in case you did not mean to.

To pick another tool, tap the round 🏠 at the top left.
