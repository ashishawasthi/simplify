Show a card

Big cards to show someone in a public place: a bus captain, station staff, a cashier, the people around.

  1. Tap a card. It fills the screen.
  2. 🔄 Turn around turns it for the person opposite. 🔊 Speak plays a recorded Indian English voice, the closest Google offers to Singapore English. Words you type yourself, like a stop name, use the phone's own voice.
  3. ✕ closes it.

What the cards say 🔗

  • Seat, please: “May I have a seat please?”
  • My stop: “Please alert me when I am approaching my stop.”
  • I cannot talk: “I cannot talk now. I can point or type.”
  • Be patient with me: “Please be patient with me.”
  • Give us space, for a parent to show: “My child is overwhelmed. Please give us some space. We are OK.”
  • Thank you: “Your care is greatly appreciated. Thank you!”
💡 Seat, My stop and Thank you use the same words as LTA's Helping Hand cards, so bus captains and station staff know them.

On the set-up page, an adult can:

  • type the stop name. The card then shows “My stop: …”. Leave it empty on a shared iPad.
  • add a second line to Be patient with me: “I have a hidden disability.” or “I am autistic.”
  • turn cards off. A card turned off leaves a gap.
Telling people about a disability is the student's choice. Be patient with me has no second line unless the student wants one.
