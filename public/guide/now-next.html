Now and next

Pictures of what happens now, and what comes next. Knowing what is coming makes a change easier.

  1. Tap ＋ Add. Tap a picture. You can change the words under it. Tap ✔ Done.
  2. To add more cards, tap My day at the top, then ＋ Add. Up to 12 cards.
  3. When the Now card is finished, tap ✔ Done. The Next card moves up to Now.

The ⏳ on the Now card opens Wait.

My day 🔗

My day shows the whole list, top to bottom. Cards that are done are small and grey, with ✔.

  • ↑ ↓ move a card up or down.
  • Changed marks a card whose plan has changed. It shows in both views, so the change is said, not hidden.
  • Tap a card's picture to pick another one.
  • ✕ takes a card away. Tap Put it back if you did not mean to.
  • Tap a card with ✔ to make it Now again.
💡 The round ✕ at the top right starts the day again. The cards stay.
