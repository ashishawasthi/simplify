ℹ️ How to use this app

Simplify — a step-by-step guide

This app answers one question: do I have enough money to buy what I want? It is free, private, and works without internet.

💰 1. Put in your money 🔗

Tap the My money box at the top and type how much money you have. That is all you need to do — the answer works itself out at the bottom of the screen.

Not sure how much you have? Tap the round 💵 button next to My money. Then tap a picture for each note and coin in your wallet. The app adds them up for you. Tap ✔ Done when you finish.

The 💵 button only shows while the money box is empty. To count your notes again, tap the ✕ in the box first.

🛒 2. Add the things you want to buy 🔗

Under Things I want to buy, type the price of the thing you want. The price is on the tag or the shelf.

Want more than one thing? Tap ＋ Add another thing and type the next price. That button only appears once the last price box has something in it, so you never end up with empty boxes.

✅ 3. Read the answer 🔗

The answer is always at the bottom of the screen. It updates by itself — you do not need to press anything.

✅ Green means YES — you have enough money. It also shows how much money you will have left.
✋ Red means NOT ENOUGH — it shows how much more money you need.

Made a mistake in one box? Tap the ✕ inside that box to empty it. To clear everything at once, tap the round ✕ next to Start over at the top. Either way a Put it back button appears for a few seconds, in case you did not mean to.

🎤 Speak instead of typing 🔗

You do not have to type. Every empty money box shows a 🎤 button inside it. You can say the money out loud.

  1. Tap the 🎤 inside an empty money box. A small Tap microphone on keyboard, then say window opens, and the keyboard appears.
  2. Tap the microphone 🎤 button on the keyboard.
  3. Say the amount. For example: “ten dollars fifty”.
  4. The window shows the money it heard. Tap ✔ Done and the number goes into the box.

Once a number is in the box, the 🎤 turns into a ✕. Want to say a new amount? Tap the ✕ to empty the box and the 🎤 comes back.

💡 It is okay to say it your own way — “ten fifty”, “10 dollars and 50 cents” and “$10.50” all work.

Cannot see a microphone on your keyboard? There is help at the end of this guide.

📱 Put the app on your home screen 🔗

Add the app to your phone's home screen. Then it opens with one tap, like a normal app — even without internet.

iPhone or iPad (Safari) 🔗

  1. Open simplify.whiz.coach in the Safari app.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome) 🔗

  1. Open simplify.whiz.coach in the Chrome app.
  2. Tap the menu button — the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Tap Add (or Install).

Now look at your home screen. Tap the new Simplify icon any time.

💡 The app remembers your numbers. Count your money at home, then check the answer at the shop — no internet needed.

🎤 No microphone on your keyboard? 🔗

The microphone belongs to your keyboard, not to this app. Find your keyboard below and follow the steps.

Gboard (most Android phones) 🔗

  1. Look at the row of small buttons just above the letters. Tap the round microphone at the right end.
  2. If it is not there, open your phone's Settings. Tap Apps, then Gboard, then Permissions, then Microphone. Tap Allow.
🔗 Google's help page for Gboard

Samsung keyboard 🔗

  1. Look at the row of small buttons just above the letters. Tap the microphone.
  2. If you cannot see that row, press and hold the comma key next to the space bar.
  3. To switch it on, open Settings. Tap General management, then Samsung Keyboard settings, then Voice input.
🔗 Samsung's help page for the keyboard

Microsoft SwiftKey 🔗

  1. Tap the microphone on the row above the letters, or press and hold the comma key.
  2. To switch it on, open the SwiftKey app. Tap Rich input, then Voice input key.
🔗 Microsoft's help page for SwiftKey

The first time you tap the microphone, your phone may ask whether the keyboard can listen. Tap While using the app.

💡 You never have to use the microphone. Typing the number always works.

🔒 Your money stays private 🔗

Everything stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no account and no sign-in.
